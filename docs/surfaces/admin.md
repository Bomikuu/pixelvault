# Administration surface brief

**Mode:** Operate. **Status:** proposed staff workspace; not part of public social profiles.

**Job:** resolve one named operational case with minimal necessary data and a traceable reason. Lead with queues needing action, not a decorative metric wall.

**Routes:** `/admin/queues`, `/admin/verifications/:id`, `/admin/registrations/:id`, `/admin/cases/:id`, `/admin/reconciliation`, `/admin/commissions`, `/admin/templates`, `/admin/policies`, `/admin/audit`.

**First viewport:** queue counts by actionable status and filtered work rows. Case detail shows subject, status, submitted evidence, existing decisions and allowed next actions. Document access is purpose-limited and audited. Sensitive financial or identity decisions require step-up authentication.

**Role boundaries:** verification reviewers inspect assigned checks; support handles complaints with redacted identity evidence; moderators handle public content; finance reconciles payments and fees; administrators manage policy/version access. One staff role does not grant universal document download. Staff cannot approve their own registration or sign for a member.

**Financial workspace:** separate expected obligations, confirmed collections, reversals and known/unknown lender settlement. Commission earned, collected and reversed are distinct measures. Export uses current permissions, short-lived downloads and spreadsheet-formula neutralization.

**States:** queue empty, assignment collision, stale decision version, evidence expired, appeal open, insufficient permissions, provider outcome uncertain, unknown settlement, legal template not approved and disabled live origination. Each decision includes a reason and a retry-safe submission identity.

**Safety of operations:** no automatic debt-shaming publication, contact blasts, government referrals or silent ledger edits. Restricting new activity does not remove a member's permitted repayment, document or complaint routes.
