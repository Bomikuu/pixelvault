# Private data and retention

Public profiles allowlist display identity, chosen avatar or selected uploaded photo, coarse area, optional bio, explicitly selected eligible awards, moderated transaction-review recommendations and optional community cosmetics/points. Leaderboard participation requires opt-in and visible points; public XP describes learning and approved educational contributions, never loan or repayment quality. Registration email and mobile are private and are not public handles. Income, document keys, precise residential locations, debt balances and adverse records are private. Staff roles and assignment reauthorize document access; grants expire after 60 seconds and remain bound to the authenticated member.

Local evidence is Fernet-encrypted before filesystem persistence, kept out of static hosting and returned only with no-store private responses. Uploads remain quarantined until their configured scan. The delivered scanner is a named sandbox fixture, not a malware/KYC certification. The same applies to fictional seed approvals. No contact harvesting occurs.

Retention applies only to explicit due dates and respects legal holds; no undeclared default deletes required financial evidence. Production needs a reviewed retention/data-processing policy and appropriate storage/processor arrangements. Deletion of a public profile cannot erase contractual obligations or the audited financial record. Never send private documents/messages to Jev or graph extraction services.

Mapbox receives map-view requests and the city/area text a person deliberately enters into place search. Account identity, verification evidence and loan records are not added to those requests. Search excludes address results and rejects street-number/personal-looking input; temporary geocoding results are not persisted. Public map markers use server-provided approximate area centroids. Activity streams publish only revision changes derived from publicly visible offers, not private financial or message events.

Own contact settings distinguish saved email/mobile from channel ownership. Neither SMS nor email verification delivery is configured. Contact changes create a new evidence-needed record so old contact approval cannot qualify new details. Profile-photo uploads accept bounded JPEG/PNG/WebP, reject animation and risky dimensions, and re-encode into metadata-free JPEG at no more than 768px. An opaque current-photo endpoint serves only a current active/non-suspended member’s selected photo; stored-photo preview in avatar mode is owner-only. Removing a photo clears its current bytes and identifier. Arbitrary remote photo URLs and evidence documents are never used as profile pictures.

Co-borrower invitations are private. Creators attest to permission before entering another person’s contact details, receive a signed seven-day link and share it themselves. No automatic message or Facebook lookup occurs. Unclaimed incoming invitations redact creator-supplied phone/Facebook; accepting requires the account’s own registered mobile, current identity/contact evidence and explicit sharing consent. Once accepted, only inviter/invitee member IDs authorize access, including after email reassignment. Lenders receive identity and current consent status for a linked application, never these contact fields. Decline, cancellation or consent withdrawal clears the shared mobile/Facebook values. Pending-link expiry does not imply an automated retention purge. An invitation never grants loan/document access or creates joint liability.

Offer comments are author-private pending moderation; held private/financial allegations cannot be approved. Likes and eligible achievement reactions do not add XP or establish creditworthiness. Public reaction reads recheck current award evidence, and transaction recommendation counts recheck qualifying published reviews. Production retention and processor decisions remain subject to the same launch gate above.
