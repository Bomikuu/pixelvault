# Incident response procedure

1. Restrict new origination and affected provider routes while retaining permitted repayment, document and complaint access.
2. Preserve scoped operation IDs, immutable event IDs and audit metadata. Do not dump document bodies, credentials or private messages into logs.
3. Classify the incident: unauthorized access, identity evidence, duplicate/uncertain collection, unsupported settlement, compromised secret or service outage. Assign a named owner and record the case.
4. Revoke/rotate affected session/provider/encryption keys using a reviewed recovery procedure. Maintain access to historical encrypted evidence needed for legal holds.
5. Reconcile source facts before appending corrections. Never delete ledger history or publicly accuse a member because a provider failed.
6. Follow applicable notification obligations with qualified review and verified facts. Record the actual outcome and add a targeted regression test.

This document is a procedure, not evidence of a completed incident drill or regulatory approval.
